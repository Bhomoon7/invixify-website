Useful answers

Frequently asked questions

What to expect before we map your first build.

What is an AI call agent?

An AI call agent is a voice assistant that answers your business phone line, talks with callers naturally, and takes action — booking appointments into your calendar, taking messages, or routing urgent calls to your team. We build ours around your services, your hours, and your rules.

Does the AI actually sound human?

It's designed to sound natural and conversational — not robotic. The best proof is hearing it: book a 15-minute demo and we'll let you listen to it handle a live call.

What happens when the AI can't answer a caller?

It doesn't guess. When a call falls outside what you've approved, the agent takes a detailed message or hands the call off to your team, following the escalation rules we set up with you.

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

Every build is scoped around your call volume, workflows, and integrations, so we don't publish a one-size-fits-all price. Book a 15-minute demo, tell us what you need, and we'll give you a clear quote.

How long does setup take?

It depends on the scope — a straightforward call agent is faster than a multi-workflow build. We map the timeline with you on the demo call before anything starts, so there are no surprises.

Do you only work with HVAC companies?

HVAC is where we started, and we know the trade well — after-hours calls, dispatch, seasonal rushes. We also build for plumbers, roofers, solar installers, and other service businesses across the USA, UK, and Canada.

What does the website chatbot do?

It answers the questions you approve, collects visitor details, and guides qualified visitors toward booking, contacting you, or a human handoff — while they're still on your site and interested.

How do we get started?

Book a 15-minute demo at https://cal.com/15-minutes-demo. We'll walk through what keeps repeating in your business and show you exactly what we'd automate first.
