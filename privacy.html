Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Invixify ("we", "us") is an AI automation agency. This page explains, in plain language, what information we collect through invixify.me and what we do with it.

Information you share with us

  • Direct email: when you write to contact@invixify.me, we receive your name, email address, and message so we can reply.
  • Demo bookings: when you book a call through our scheduling page, we receive the details you enter (name, email, and anything you add) so we can hold the meeting.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing.
  • We don't run accounts, profiles, or advertising trackers on this site.
  • We don't collect information about you beyond what you choose to send us.

How we use your information

Only to respond to your inquiry, hold your booking, and — if you become a client — deliver the work. That's it.

Data retention

We keep correspondence for as long as it's useful for the conversation or the client relationship, then delete it on request.

Your rights

Write to contact@invixify.me any time to ask what we hold about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
